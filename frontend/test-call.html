Things to try

  • "මට සැලෝන් එකක් තියෙනවා. TIKIRII මට මොකද කරන්නේ?" (the agent explains what it does for a salon)
  • "What can you do for a restaurant?" / "Can you make outgoing calls?" / "Do you work with Google Calendar?"
  • "How much does it cost?" (it asks about your calls, recommends a package with its listed price, and offers a demo; it must not invent a price)
  • "I'd like a demo" (it asks your name, business and a good time, confirms your number, and saves a demo request)
  • "Please send me the details by message" (the agent confirms your number first).
  • "Are you a robot?" (it says honestly that it is an AI voice agent)
  • Press 2, then speak Sinhala: the agent should keep to Tamil.
  • Interrupt the agent while it talks, or stay silent to hear "are you still there?".
